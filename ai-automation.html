FAQ

More questions? Ask us on WhatsApp.

What can AI automation do for a small business?

It can reply to enquiries, follow up leads, send reminders, read invoices and documents, and move data between your tools, so your team spends less time on repetitive work.

What is the difference between n8n, Make and Zapier?

All three connect apps into workflows. Zapier is simplest, Make is more visual and flexible, and n8n can be self-hosted and suits complex or high-volume flows. We pick the right one for your needs.

What is a RAG chatbot?

A RAG chatbot answers questions using your own documents, such as manuals, policies or product sheets, so its answers stay accurate to your business. It usually takes about a week to set up.

Is my data safe?

Yes. We use official APIs, limit access to what each workflow needs, and can run automations on infrastructure you control.
