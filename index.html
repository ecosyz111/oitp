FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still unsure? Ask us on WhatsApp.

How much does a website cost?

Business websites start from ₹25,000. The final price depends on the pages and features you need (booking, payments, WhatsApp, catalogue), and you get a clear quote before work starts.

How fast can my website go live?

Most business websites go live in 2–5 days. After you book, the first draft reaches you in 24–48 hours.

What is the ₹500 booking?

₹500 books your build slot. It is not an extra charge: it is adjusted in full into your final price, and it comes back in full if the first draft isn't for you.

Do you build apps and MVPs for startups?

Yes. Web apps, mobile apps or both, with logins, dashboards, payments and admin panels. Bigger products are scoped with you before we quote.

Can you add WhatsApp, booking or payments to my site?

Yes. WhatsApp chat, enquiry forms, Google Maps, online booking and payment gateways are all things we set up regularly.

Do you help after the website is live?

Yes. We offer ongoing support and updates, and can add software, AI automation or marketing as your business grows.
